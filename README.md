# Airflow Representation in Hygrothermal Modelling for Moisture Risk Assessment of Building Envelopes: A Critical Review of 45 years
This repository provides the data used in the systematic analysis for the review paper "Airflow Representation in Hygrothermal Modelling for Moisture Risk Assessment of Building Envelopes: A Critical Review of 45 Years" by A. Sarkany et al.

The dataset is intended to support the review process and will be made publicly available in an open repository such as Zenodo after this paper is accepted. The data will be accompanied by appropriate metadata in accordance with the FAIR principles to ensure findability, accessibility, interoperability, and reusability.
